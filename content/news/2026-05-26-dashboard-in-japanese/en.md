The Dashboard can now be shown in Japanese. Navigation, buttons, settings and page descriptions appear in Japanese wherever a translation is available.

## What changes

- **Language on your first visit**: When you first open the [Dashboard]({{.PlatformAPIURL}}), it is shown in Japanese if your browser's language settings include Japanese, and in English otherwise.
- **Switching language**: In the sidebar shown outside a Project, open Settings under Other. Under Account you will find Language settings. Choose English or 日本語 there and the display changes straight away.
- **Docs**: Docs in the sidebar is also shown in the language you choose.

## What you need to do

Nothing is required. If you want a different display language, choose it again under Language settings.

## Good to know

- Text without a translation stays in English.
- Values in input fields, code, and technical values such as URLs and SQL are not translated.
- Your choice is saved in the browser you are using. In a different browser, the language is chosen again from that browser's language settings.
