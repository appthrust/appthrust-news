AppThrust is now available to Craftsman.

In AppThrust, each Project chooses and installs apps from the marketplace. Project members sign in to use the installed apps, and Home lists all of them in one place.

Apps are added and updated from the marketplace in the [Dashboard]({{.PlatformAPIURL}}). The news item "Apps available in the marketplace" summarises what is on offer.

Upcoming releases and maintenance will be announced here in AppThrust News.
